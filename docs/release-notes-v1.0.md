## Obex 1.0: Instagram, without the scroll

The first public release. 🎉

**What's in it**
- 💬 Messages: DMs, group chats, photos, videos and voice messages
- ⭕ Stories: your friends' stories (with sound on), and no feed underneath
- 🔍 Search: people, posts and videos
- 👤 Profiles
- 🚫 No home feed, no Reels feed, no Explore, and no swiping from one reel to the next
- 🔒 Optional Instagram lock: opening the real Instagram app (or tapping its notifications) opens Obex instead

**Install**
1. Download **`Obex.apk`** below, on your Android phone (Android 10+).
2. Open it and allow your browser to install unknown apps if asked. On Samsung, turn off *Auto Blocker* first if the install is blocked.
3. Open Obex and sign in to Instagram.
